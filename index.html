WHAT WE CAN HELP WITH

Practical help when you need it most.

Hospital Buddy provides non-medical assistance for patients and accompanying family members.

🧭

Hospital Navigation

Help finding permitted departments, counters, registration areas and hospital facilities.

📄

Registration & Paperwork

Practical assistance with forms, copies and routine administrative procedures.

📅

Appointment Assistance

Help understanding and following official registration and appointment procedures.

🗣️

Language Assistance

Basic non-medical support in available regional languages.

👴

Elderly Assistance

Practical support for elderly patients where hospital rules permit.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Support

Assistance for family members accompanying patients during hospital visits.

⚠️ Important

Hospital Buddy does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact the hospital emergency department or emergency services immediately.
