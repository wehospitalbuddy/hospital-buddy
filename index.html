OUR SERVICES

Practical assistance, not medical care

Hospital Buddies provide practical support to help patients and families navigate hospital-related tasks.

🧭

Hospital Navigation

Help finding departments, counters, registration areas and other hospital locations.

📅

Appointment Assistance

Practical assistance with appointment procedures and related hospital processes.

🗂️

Administrative Help

Assistance with forms, counters, document movement and other non-medical administrative tasks.

🗣️

Language Assistance

Communicate more comfortably with help from a Buddy who understands your language.

👴

Elderly Assistance

Practical assistance for elderly patients and accompanying family members.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Support

Help family members manage practical hospital tasks during a visit.
