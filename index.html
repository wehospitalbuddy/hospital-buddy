WHAT CAN MY BUDDY HELP WITH?

Practical help when you need it

Hospital Buddy provides practical, non-medical assistance for patients and their families.

🧭

Hospital Navigation

Help finding departments, counters, registration areas, wards and other hospital locations.

📋

Registration & Paperwork

Practical assistance with forms, registration procedures and administrative processes.

👥

Accompany You

A Buddy can accompany patients or family members during practical hospital-related tasks.

💳

Payment & Document Guidance

Guidance about where to go for payments, documents and administrative counters.

🗣

Language Assistance

Communicate more comfortably with help from a Buddy who understands your language.

👴

Support for Elderly

Practical assistance for elderly patients and family members during hospital visits.
